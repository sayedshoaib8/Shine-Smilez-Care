Restorative Implantology

1. Dental Implants

Dental implants are permanent fixtures fabricated from biocompatible titanium that integrate with the jawbone to replace missing roots. They provide structural stability for crowns, bridges, or full-arch prosthetics.

Who may consider implants: Adults with single or multiple missing teeth, good overall general health, and sufficient jawbone density to anchor an implant.

Our consultation process: Diagnostic clinical imaging, assessment of neighboring teeth and gums, and an in-depth conversation about timelines and restoration options.

Orthodontic Options

2. Clear Aligners & 3. Braces

Crooked teeth, gaps, and bite discrepancies can impact chewing ease and oral hygiene. We provide both clear aligners (removable transparent trays) and fixed braces (traditional or ceramic).

Clear Aligners: Ideal for patients seeking a discreet cosmetic appearance. Removable during meals and routine tooth brushing.

Orthodontic Braces: Highly effective for addressing complex tooth rotations, severe spacing, and jaw alignment corrections.

Consult On Aligners & Braces
Tooth Preservation & Surgery

4. Root Canal & 5. Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Root Canal Treatment (RCT): Designed to relieve pain caused by severe dental infection, deep decay, or cracked tooth pulp. The infected inner tissue is cleaned, disinfected, and securely sealed.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction: Led by Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon Dr. Sudhanshu Joshi, third molars that are impacted, misaligned, or causing pressure on adjacent teeth are evaluated and removed with gentle operative techniques.
