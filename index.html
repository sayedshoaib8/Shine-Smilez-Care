Clinical Treatments & Oral Care

From routine dental preservation to advanced oral maxillofacial surgery and smile alignment, our practice delivers focused care tailored to your oral health.

1. Dental Implants

Sturdy, natural-feeling tooth replacement solutions designed to restore chewing function and aesthetic stability for single or multiple missing teeth.

2. Aligners

Custom clear aligner trays that gently shift your teeth into proper alignment without conspicuous metal wires or brackets.

3. Braces

Traditional and ceramic orthodontic braces for addressing dental crowding, bite spacing, and complex malocclusions with clinical precision.

4. Root Canal Treatment

Specialized restorative therapy designed to treat infected or severely decayed inner tooth pulp, relieving discomfort and preserving natural teeth.

5. Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Safe surgical removal of impacted or painful wisdom teeth under expert maxillofacial surgical supervision with thorough post-operative support.

6. Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Surgical expertise addressing complex jaw discrepancies, cysts, facial trauma evaluations, and challenging oral pathologies in Wadala.

7. Cosmetic Dentistry

Aesthetic tooth reshaping, composite bonding, porcelain veneers, and enamel brightening to enhance smile harmony and balance.

8. Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle and reassuring dental check-ups, sealants, and cavity treatments tailored to help children maintain healthy growing smiles.

9. Snoring / Sleep Solutions

Custom-fabricated oral appliances to support airway clearance during sleep, helping reduce nighttime snoring in appropriate candidates.

Featured Care Spotlight

Dental Implants

Dental implants provide a sturdy, long-term foundation for artificial teeth. Fabricated from biocompatible titanium, the implant acts as a prosthetic tooth root that anchors securely into your jawbone.

Who may consider them: Patients missing one or more teeth looking for stable biting power without altering adjacent teeth.
Consultation process: Thorough clinical examination, radiographic imaging of jawbone volume, and step-by-step treatment mapping.
Professional evaluation: Proper surgical assessment ensures optimal implant positioning and long-term restorative health.
